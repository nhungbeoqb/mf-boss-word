One word for the boss: **Parrot** — the boss who repeats your idea in the meeting as their own. You pitch it on Tuesday; by Thursday he is squawking it back at you in front of the whole room, as if it hatched in his head.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 6 bytes, no trailing newline).
